---
title: A. Glossary
---

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Physical AI** | Artificial intelligence integrated into a physical body that interacts with the real world. |
| **Kinematics** | The study of motion without regard to forces that cause the motion. |
| **Dynamics** | The study of motion and its causes (forces/torques). |
| **ROS 2** | Robot Operating System — a middleware framework for robotics development. |
| **Sim-to-Real** | The process of training a robot in simulation and deploying it to hardware. |
| **VLA** | Vision-Language-Action models — neural networks that map visual/language inputs to robotic actions. |
| **ZMP** | Zero Moment Point — a concept for balancing humanoid robots. |
