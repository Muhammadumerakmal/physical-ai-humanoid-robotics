# Further Reading & Resources

A curated map of where to go next. Everything here is grouped by the part of the
book it extends, and annotated so you know *why* it's worth your time rather than
just *that* it exists. Start with the **foundational** items in each group; the
rest are there when you need depth.

## Books — the shelf worth owning

| Book | Why it matters | Best for |
| ---- | -------------- | -------- |
| Lynch & Park, *Modern Robotics* | The clearest modern treatment of kinematics, dynamics, and control. Free PDF + video lectures. | Parts I, III |
| Thrun, Burgard & Fox, *Probabilistic Robotics* | The canonical reference for state estimation, filtering, and SLAM. | Part II |
| Sutton & Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* | The RL textbook. Free online. Read it before any RL paper. | Part IV |
| Spong, Hutchinson & Vidyasagar, *Robot Modeling and Control* | A rigorous classical-robotics complement to *Modern Robotics*. | Part III |
| Siciliano & Khatib (eds.), *Springer Handbook of Robotics* | The encyclopedia — a reference, not a read-through. | All parts |
| Murphy, *Introduction to AI Robotics* | A readable history of the perception–action paradigm. | Part I |

## Courses & lecture series

- **Underactuated Robotics** (Russ Tedrake, MIT) — the best course on dynamics,
  trajectory optimization, and control for legged/agile robots.
- **CS287 / Deep RL** (Berkeley) and **Spinning Up in Deep RL** (OpenAI) — a
  gentle, code-first on-ramp to modern reinforcement learning.
- **ROS 2 official tutorials** — the fastest path from zero to a running node,
  publisher/subscriber, and a simulated robot.
- **NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute** — hands-on Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab labs.

## Landmark & primary-source papers

Grouped so you can follow a thread rather than a reading list.

**Learning & control**
- *Emergence of Locomotion Behaviours in Rich Environments* (DeepMind) — RL from
  simple rewards produces surprisingly capable locomotion.
- *Learning to Walk in Minutes Using Massively Parallel Deep RL* (ETH Zürich) —
  the sim-to-real recipe behind modern quadruped locomotion.
- *Sim-to-Real Transfer with Domain Randomization* (OpenAI, dexterous hand) — why
  randomizing simulation makes policies survive contact with reality.

**Vision–language–action & foundation models**
- *RT-2: Vision-Language-Action Models* (Google DeepMind) — internet-scale
  knowledge transferred into robot actions.
- *Open X-Embodiment / RT-X* — the argument (and dataset) for training one policy
  across many robot bodies.
- Figure AI, *Helix* — a readable industry write-up of a generalist humanoid VLA.

## Datasets

- **Open X-Embodiment** — the largest open collection of real robot trajectories.
- **DROID** — a large, diverse manipulation dataset for imitation learning.
- **KITTI / nuScenes** — perception and state-estimation benchmarks (autonomous
  driving, but the sensors and problems transfer).
- **EGO4D / Ego-Exo4D** — egocentric human video, increasingly used to bootstrap
  manipulation policies.

## Communities & staying current

- **ROS Discourse** and the **Robotics Stack Exchange** — where practitioners
  actually answer setup and design questions.
- **Hugging Face LeRobot** — open models, datasets, and a friendly community for
  robot learning.
- Conference proceedings worth skimming each year: **CoRL**, **RSS**, **ICRA**,
  and **IROS**.

:::tip How to use this list
Don't read top-to-bottom. Pick the one book and one course for the part you're
currently in, and treat the papers as follow-ups when a chapter leaves you
wanting the primary source. Depth in one thread beats breadth across ten.
:::
