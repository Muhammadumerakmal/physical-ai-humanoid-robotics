# Is Kitaab Ke Baare Mein

## AI ka agla decade ek jism rakhta hai

Satr saal tak, artificial intelligence sheeshe ke peeche rahi. Usne chess kheli,
tasveerein chhaantin, aur — haal hi mein — essay aur code likha. Lekin usne kabhi
girta hua glass nahi pakra, dhalaan par apna balance nahi sambhala, aur na yeh
faisla kiya ke ande ko kitni sakhti se pakarna hai. Woh daur khatam ho raha hai.
AI ki sarhad data center se nikal kar **aise jismon mein daakhil ho rahi hai jo
mehsoos karte, faisla lete aur asli duniya mein amal karte hain**.

Yeh hai **Physical AI**: woh maqaam jahan machine learning, robotics, aur control
theory milti hain. Aur iski sab se bari misaal **humanoid** hai — ek machine jo
hamari tarah bani ho, do taangon par chalti ho, do haathon se kaam karti ho, aur
us duniya mein kaam karti ho jo insani jism ke liye banayi gayi hai.

Masla yeh hai ke embodiment un tareeqon se *mushkil* hai jin se digital AI kabhi
nahi thi. Asli duniya shor bhari, musalsal, be-reham hai aur real time mein chalti
hai — undo ka koi option nahi. Ek model jo notebook mein 99% durust ho, phir bhi
munh ke bal gir sakta hai. Us khaai ko paatna — saaf code se le kar aisi machine
tak jo seedhi khari rahe — ek hunar hai, aur yeh kitaab ise bunyaadi usoolon se le
kar ek chalte hue robot tak sikhati hai.

:::tip Yeh kitaab kis tarah mukhtalif hai
Yeh sirf theory par khatam nahi hoti. Iska ikhtitaam ek interactive humanoid par
hota hai jise aap saade alfaaz mein hukm dete hain, seedha apne browser mein — har
baab ke perception, control aur learning ke tasawwuraat ek hi jism mein juray hue.
Har safhe par ek **AI tutor** bhi maujood hai, jo khud is kitaab par mabni hai.
:::

## Yeh Kitaab Kya Hai

Ek zaheen machine ke poore stack ke liye wahid mustanad zariya. Ismein shaamil hai:

- Physical AI ki **bunyaadein**: embodied intelligence digital AI se kyun mukhtalif
  hai, aur woh riyazi jo sensing ko amal se jorti hai.
- **Perception**: ek robot kaise dekhta, mehsoos karta aur apni jagah ka andaza
  rakhta hai.
- **Actuation aur control**: motors, kinematics, aur woh control loops jo ek biped
  ko seedha khara rakhti hain.
- **Learning aur autonomy**: machine learning, reinforcement learning, imitation,
  aur woh vision-language-action models jo robots ko hidayat ki satah ki khudmukhtaari
  dete hain.
- **Systems aur deployment**: software stack, ROS 2, simulation, aur ek robot asli
  duniya mein kaise pohnchta hai.

## Yeh Kitaab Kis Ke Liye Hai

- **Nawaamoz AI engineers** — aap models aur data jaante hain; yeh kitaab unhein
  jismon aur physics par laagu karna sikhati hai.
- **Robotics mein aane wale software developers** — aap pehle se code likhte hain;
  yeh kitaab neeche ki physical satah barhati hai.
- **Talaba** — bunyaadi usoolon se le kar ek chalte hue humanoid tak ek munazzam
  raasta, har baab ke aakhir mein mashqon ke saath.

## Aap Kya Kar Sakein Ge

Kitaab ke ikhtitaam par aap yeh kar sakein ge:

- Ek physical-AI system ka poora architecture bayan karna — sensors se le kar
  actuators tak.
- **Python** aur **ROS 2** mein robot code likhna aur chalana.
- Ek simulation set up karna, policy train karna, aur use hardware par deploy karna.
- Ek asli task ke liye perception, control aur learning ke tareeqon mein se chunna.
- Ek saada robot system shuru se aakhir tak design aur evaluate karna.

## Apna Raasta Chunein

Kitaab ek seedhe mutaalay ke tor par kaam karti hai, lekin aap darmiyaan mein bhi
kood sakte hain:

- **Seedha padhein** (tajweez karda) — Part I–VI ko tarteeb se parhein.
- **Pehle banayein** — shuru se hi amali kaam: systems ke baab jaldi dekh lein aur
  bunyaadon par baad mein wapas aayein.
- **Software se tez daakhla** — seedha learning aur systems ke hisson mein jaayein,
  phir sensing aur control bharein.
- **Research par tawajjuh** — learning aur mustaqbil ke hisson se shuru karein, phir
  perception aur control mein gehraai mein jaayein.

Har baab **mashqon** par khatam hota hai jo parhne ko banane mein badal deti hain.

## Kitaab Ki Tarteeb

Chhe hisse aur appendices; har hissa stack ki ek tehri hai:

- **Part I — Foundations**: Physical AI kya hai, robotics ki bunyaadein, riyazi ki
  kit, aur humanoid architecture.
- **Part II — Sensing & Perception**: sensors aur actuators, perception, computer
  vision, aur state estimation.
- **Part III — Actuation & Control**: kinematics aur dynamics, motion control,
  balance aur locomotion, aur manipulation.
- **Part IV — Learning & Intelligence**: machine learning, reinforcement learning,
  imitation, VLA models, LLMs, aur AI agents.
- **Part V — Systems, Simulation & Deployment**: ROS 2, software stack, simulation,
  Isaac Sim, aur asli duniya mein deployment.
- **Part VI — The Road Ahead**: humanoid robotics ka mustaqbil.
- **Appendices**: glossary, resources, environment setup, riyazi ka hawala, aur
  project ideas.
